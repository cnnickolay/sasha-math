⚖️ Balance the Equation

⚖️ Balanced — both sides are equal
🎈 What is a balloon?
  • A weight pushes its pan DOWN ⬇️
  • A balloon is a minus (like −3). It pulls its pan UP ⬆️
  • You can't put "minus weight" on a scale — but a balloon lifts, so it works! 🪄
  • A weight + the same balloon = 0 → they pull equally and float away together
  • So to remove a −3: drop a +3 weight on it. Then do the same to the other pan to keep it fair ⚖️
